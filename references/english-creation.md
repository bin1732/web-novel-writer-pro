## 英文网文创作标准

### Chapter Structure (章节结构)

```markdown
## Chapter [Number]: [Chapter Title]
- Target word count: 2000-3500 words
- Opening hook (first 3 sentences): Must grab attention
  - Hook types: Question / Shock / Action / Mystery / Emotional
- Body: 3-5 scenes with rising tension
- Cliffhanger ending: Last paragraph must create "need to know"
  - Cliffhanger types: Revelation / Danger / Choice / Twist / Question
```

### Story Arc Standards (故事弧标准)

| Element | Standard |
|---------|----------|
| Opening Hook | First 3 paragraphs must hook reader |
| Inciting Incident | Within first chapter (or first 15% for longer works) |
| First Major Plot Point | By 25% mark |
| Midpoint Twist | Around 50% |
| Climax | 85-90% mark |
| Resolution | 90-100% (satisfying but can leave room for sequel) |

### Character Standards

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Protagonist | Relatable flaw + clear desire + active agency |
| Love Interest (Romance) | Meet within first 25% / chemistry scene within first 50% |
| Antagonist | Believable motivation / not evil for evil's sake |
| Supporting Cast | Each has a function: plot driver / theme mirror / comic relief |

### Pacing Standards

| Section | Pacing |
|---------|--------|
| First 10 chapters | Fast, establish hook and stakes |
| Middle chapters | Medium, develop subplots and characters |
| Pre-climax chapters | Accelerating tension |
| Climax | Maximum intensity |

## 英文自然度优化规则

### High-Frequency AI Words to Avoid (英文版禁用词)

| Category | Words to Avoid | Replace With |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| **Overused Transitions** | However, Therefore, Moreover, Furthermore, Consequently | But, So, And, Then, (start fresh sentence) |
| **Empty Intensifiers** | Very, Really, Extremely, Absolutely, Completely | Specific descriptors or delete |
| **Cliché Emotions** | He felt angry / She was sad / They were excited | Show through action and dialogue |
| **Overly Formal** | In order to, Due to the fact that, In the event of | To, Because, If |
| **AI Sentence Starters** | It is important to note that, It is worth mentioning that, It should be noted that | Delete entirely, start with the fact |
| **Hedge Words** | Perhaps, Maybe, Might, Possibly, Seemingly | Commit or delete |
| **Perfect Description** | Overly balanced descriptions of every scene | Leave gaps, let reader imagine |

### English Natural-Writing Techniques (英文自然度优化技法)

| Technique | Before (AI) | After (Human) |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Irrelevant Detail** | "He walked into the room and checked the documents." | "He walked into the room. The blinds were crooked. He checked the documents." |
| **Imperfect Reaction** | "She immediately understood the implication." | "It took her a second. Then another. 'Wait—' she said." |
| **Broken Dialogue** | "'What are you doing?' 'I'm checking the files.' 'Okay.'" | "'What are you—' 'Files.' 'What?' 'Checking.'" |
| **Uneven Pacing** | Every paragraph same length | Mix: 1-sentence punch paragraphs + longer descriptive paragraphs |
| **Flawed Character** | "He analyzed the situation logically." | "He guessed. Wrong. Guessed again. Wrong again. Third time, maybe." |

### Natural Writing Strategy

```
Layer 1: Structural Variety (结构多样性)
├── Vary sentence length (short 5-word vs long 40-word mix)
├── Vary paragraph length (1-sentence punch + long descriptive)
└── Vary chapter length (don't make every chapter exactly the same length)

Layer 2: Stylistic Naturalness (风格自然度)
├── Use personal idioms and quirks
├── Include "imperfect" descriptions
├── Leave some threads unresolved (life is messy)
└── Add specific, concrete details (not generic "the room" but "the room with peeling wallpaper")

Layer 3: Content Originality (内容原创性)
├── Show don't tell emotions
├── Include unexpected character reactions
├── Use dialect and register shifts
└── Add sensory details (smell/touch/taste)
```

## 平台签约/出版指南

### Wattpad Publishing Path

1. Write and post chapters (build readership)
2. Apply to Wattpad Stars program (after 10K+ reads)
3. Participate in Watty Awards (annual competition)
4. Get discovered by Wattpad Books (traditional publishing path) or Wattpad WEBTOON Studios (adaptation path)

### Amazon KDP Self-Publishing

1. Write complete book (50K-100K words recommended for debut)
2. Format and design cover
3. Publish on KDP (choose KU enrollment for max visibility)
4. Build mailing list for launch
5. Run Amazon Ads (start small, test keywords)

### Webnovel Contract

1. Write first 10-20 chapters for review
2. Apply for a contract when reaching 30K+ words with consistent quality
3. Meet weekly word count requirements (typically 10K-20K words/week)
4. Engage with readers via Power Stones and comments

## 英文写作常见陷阱（中英文作者专属）

中文母语者写英文网文时容易犯以下错误：

| 陷阱 | 问题 | 改前 | 改后 |
|------|------|------|------|
| **过长的句子** | 中文可以一串逗号到底，英文不行 | "He walked into the room, looked around, saw the letter on the table, picked it up, and read it carefully." | "He walked into the room. The letter sat on the table. He picked it up and read." |
| **缺少冠词** | 中文没有a/an/the概念 | "He picked up letter from table." | "He picked up the letter from the table." |
| **过度使用"very"** | 中文"非常"直译 | "He was very angry." | "His face reddened. His hands clenched into fists." |
| **对话标签冗余** | 中文"说道"习惯 | "'I don't know,' he said softly in a low voice." | "'I don't know.' His voice was barely a whisper." |
| **视角混乱** | POV切换无规则 | 一段内切换多个角色视角 | 每章锁定一个POV，只在分章处切换 |

### 英文章节结尾的10种Cliffhanger

1. **Revelation**: A shocking truth is revealed
2. **Danger**: The protagonist is in immediate peril
3. **Choice**: A difficult decision looms
4. **Twist**: Everything the reader thought is wrong
5. **Question**: A mystery is posed
6. **Arrival**: Someone/something unexpected appears
7. **Discovery**: A clue or secret is found
8. **Countdown**: Time is running out
9. **Betrayal**: Trust is broken
10. **Promise**: A vow or threat is made
